Executive Synopsis

This benchmark evaluates the generalization capabilities of SAM 3 by comparing its zero-shot segmentation performance across two highly divergent domains. RWTD tests performance on natural texture boundaries (shells, bark, sand), while CAID measures its ability to partition architectural floor plans into semantically distinct regions.
