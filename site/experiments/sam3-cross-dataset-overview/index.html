Generalization Benchmark

Executive Synopsis

This benchmark evaluates the generalization capabilities of SAM 3 by comparing its zero-shot segmentation performance across three diverse domains using Coarse Feature Clustering. RWTD tests performance on natural texture boundaries, CAID measures architectural floor plan partitioning, and STLD evaluates structural and functional segmentation.
